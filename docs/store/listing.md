## Detailed description

   dropdat lets you carry context between AI chats. Click the capsule button in
   your chat composer to capture the current conversation as a "capsule." Open
   any other supported chat and drop the capsule back in — no copy-paste, no
   re-explaining background.

**Supported sites**
- ChatGPT (chatgpt.com, chat.openai.com)
- Claude (claude.ai)
- Gemini (gemini.google.com)
- Grok (grok.com)
- Microsoft Copilot (copilot.microsoft.com)
- Perplexity (perplexity.ai)

**How it works**
1. Click the capsule button next to Voice in any supported chat.
2. Pick **Generate** to save the current chat as a capsule.
3. Pick **Drop** to open your library and inject a saved capsule into the
   composer. The capsule's title and content are sent as a single message.

**Privacy first**
Capsules live in your browser. Sign in to sync them across devices via the
dropdat dashboard. No analytics, no trackers, no remote code.
